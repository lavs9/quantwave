# Indicator Gallery

Explore the wide range of technical indicators available in QuantWave. Every indicator is optimized for both Polars batch processing and real-time streaming.

## Categories

### Native Indicators
Classic indicators like SMA, EMA, RSI, MACD, SuperTrend, and many more — all implemented in high-performance Rust.

### Ehlers DSP Suite
Advanced digital signal processing indicators by John Ehlers (Cyber Cycle, Instantaneous Trendline, Trendflex, etc.).

### Price Action & Patterns
Geometric patterns (Flags, Head & Shoulders) and market structure tools, based on rigorous MQL5 research (Parts 21, 66, 69).

### Trend & Volatility
Indicators designed to identify market trends and measure volatility.
